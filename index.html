Your brand, on the record

Ranchers registered their brands. So will you.

For generations, Western ranches recorded their brand in the state brand book, so anyone could see whose mark it was. Stated Roots carries that idea forward: your reseller brand, the channels where you sell, and the permission behind every design, kept on record.
